Our Philosophy

Three rules shape every pair that leaves the bench.

01  Start with the hide

We buy full-grain and vegetable-tanned hides, not corrected grain. The surface stays intact, so the leather breathes, creases honestly and develops a patina instead of peeling.

02  Stitch it, don't glue it

Every sole is attached with a stitched welt. It takes longer and costs more, but it means the sole can be replaced instead of the whole boot being thrown away.

03  Repair before replace

Send any ATΛL pair back within two years and we will resole, restitch or recondition it at cost. A boot that lasts is the only kind of sustainability that counts.

FAQs

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched from Karachi within 24 hours. Delivery takes 2-4 working days nationwide, and is free on orders above PKR 15,000.

Yes. Cash on delivery is available across Pakistan. You can also pay by debit or credit card at checkout.

What if the size is wrong?

You can exchange any unworn pair within 14 days. We cover the return shipping on the first exchange — just message us with your order number.

Do you repair boots you've sold?
